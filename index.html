The story, one date at a time

Our Memories

Click a date to open the story, photos, and all the little details that made that day matter.

01 22 MAR 2026 The day I first met you ＋
The very beginning

I didn't even talk to you that day.

The funny thing about our story is that the first day we met wasn't some huge movie moment. I barely spoke to you. I couldn't have known that this person standing in front of me would become such an important part of my life.

Somehow, that ordinary beginning became the starting point of something I would never want to lose. 🧿

02 04 APR 2026 The second day — the car ride ＋
The conversation that changed everything

One of the best decisions of my life.

You came to sit at the back of the car on our trip, and I remember you thinking that i am probably not going to talk to you. I was that kind of person too — quiet, awkward, not really someone who starts a conversation.

But you looked unhappy. A little sad. And somehow that was enough. I started talking to you.

It sounds like such a small thing now, but that conversation became one of the best decisions of my life. Somewhere between that car ride, those first words, and all the conversations that followed, our story really started.

03 16 MAY 2026 Leaving Bangalore… or so I thought ＋
The goodbye I thought was permanent

I honestly thought I would never come back.

Graduation was ending, I was heading back to my hometown, and in my head this was the end of my Bangalore chapter. I genuinely believed I was leaving for good.

That day we spend 6 hours together in an temple talking. Imagin telling Somone and if the bleave that.

And then life had a completely different plan. I came back — this time for my Masters — and even that was a surprise to me in its own way. What I thought was an ending quietly became another beginning.

Somewhere between leaving and returning, I realized just how much this city and you had become part of home for me.

04 30 JUL 2026 The Sunflower Surprise 🌻 ＋
The return I wanted you to remember

You came downstairs expecting nobody. I was waiting with a sunflower.

You already knew that I might come back someday, but you didn't know I was already there. I tried dropping hints, but somehow you didn't understand any of them.

At around 2:00, I called you and told you not to sleep for twenty minutes. Then I called again and started asking ridiculous questions about the weather — coffee, tea, ice cream, rain — anything that might make you wonder why I was asking.

Finally I told you to come outside. You walked down expecting no one, and there I was, holding a sunflower.

The look on your face — shocked, happy, surprised all at once — is something I don't think I will ever forget. You were just so happy.

And maybe that's why this memory means so much to me. When I left Bangalore, you cried. I never thought somebody could cry because I was leaving. That day made me realize just how much I mattered to you, and coming back to surprise you felt like giving a tiny piece of that feeling back.

05 28 AUG 2026 A Raksha Bandhan worth keeping ＋
Today & always

Some bonds deserve their own little corner of the internet.

This picture is one more chapter in a story that started so casually and somehow became family. I don't know how many more memories we'll make, but I hope there are a lot of them.

You are not just someone I happened to meet. You're one of those people who became important without asking for a place.
